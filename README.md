## From concept to container

Our typical collaboration flow:

1. **Share your brand & targets**  
   You outline your brand story, target customers, sales channels and retail or landed price ranges.

2. **Get an OEM proposal**  
   We respond with suggested bag silhouettes, paddle and gear options, materials, key features and indicative costing.

3. **Sample, test and refine**  
   We build samples; you test with your team and key customers, then refine designs, capacity, comfort and details.

4. **Lock specs and scale production**  
   Once specs are locked, we move into stable production with QC matched to your brand standards and market requirements.
